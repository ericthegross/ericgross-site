The Strip

TV series

Writer / Creator

Producer: Jon Berg

Executive producer: Robert Siegel

Co-producer: Duff McKagan

THE STRIP is a darkly funny neo-noir crime series set in the seedy underbelly of 1980s Los Angeles.

Sil Carver, a disgraced cop turned session bass player, drifts through the clubs, hustlers, and late-night excess of the Sunset Strip. When a politician’s groupie daughter goes missing, Sil takes what seems like an easy job for a quick buck. But his search draws him into a sprawling conspiracy involving developers, politicians, crooked cops, and the shadowy power brokers who really run Los Angeles.
