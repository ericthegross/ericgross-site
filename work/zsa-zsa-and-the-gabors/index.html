Zsa Zsa & the Gabors

TV series

Writer / Creator

The Gabors were the original Kardashians. Famous for being famous, but fiercer, classier, and infinitely more glamorous.

ZSA ZSA & THE GABORS is a one-hour limited series about reinvention, ambition, and the extraordinary Hungarian sisters who conquered American high society. Arriving in the United States as refugees after World War II, they armed themselves with devastating wit, dazzling diamonds, and an insatiable appetite for fame, fortune, and romance. Along the way, they seduced kings, millionaires, movie stars, and presidents, becoming pioneers of modern celebrity culture.
