Get Lite

Feature film

Original Screenplay

Release: August 2027

Set in the electrifying world of New York City’s subway dance scene, GET LITE follows a talented but sheltered teenager who discovers love, friendship, and a new sense of belonging through Litefeet, a high-energy street dance style born in Harlem.

Based on Eric Gross’s 2020 Black List screenplay, the Paramount feature is directed by Teyana Taylor and stars Storm Reid and Ceyair Wright.
