About

Eric Gross is a writer and director whose work spans film, television, and commercials.

His screenplay GET LITE was featured on the 2020 Black List and is now a Paramount feature directed by Teyana Taylor and starring Storm Reid. His Mr. T biopic T appeared on the 2019 Black List and was acquired by Lionsgate.

He’s also written and directed original short films and comedy series, and worked with brands including Nike, Apple, Netflix, and Spotify.

He likes bad dogs, Tom Waits, and plays too much chess.
